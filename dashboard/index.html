VALUATION MODEL

How the radar ranks.

Implied value estimates a market capitalization using the sealed snapshot’s TVL, comparable token market caps, seven-day TVL change and tracking age.

Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth factor × maturity factor

01 / Peer multiple

Take the median of individual market cap ÷ TVL ratios among tokenized protocols with positive market cap and TVL. Categories with fewer than peers use the global median of from peers.

02 / Growth factor

Clamp seven-day TVL change to −50 through +50 percentage points. Divide by 200 and add 1, giving 0.75× to 1.25×. Missing growth gives 1.00×.

03 / Maturity factor

Use days from listing to snapshot, clamped at zero and 1,095. Divide by 1,095, multiply by 0.20 and add 1. Missing age gives 1.00×. The factor ranges from 1.00× to 1.20×.

Round to the nearest dollar with a $1 minimum. Ties use slug order. Listing age measures days tracked by DefiLlama.
